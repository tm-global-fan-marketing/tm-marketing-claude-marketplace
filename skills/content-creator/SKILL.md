description: Use this skill when a marketer needs a coordinated SET of B2B marketing assets for a single campaign, product, or theme — not a single piece of copy. Triggers on requests like "I need a campaign for [product] with a one-sheet, LinkedIn posts, and an email" or "build me B2B assets for [theme]" where the audience is clients, client prospects, or B2B2C reach through a channel such as LinkedIn, and where two or more of the following are requested: blog, case study, LinkedIn post, newsletter inclusion, single-send email, web page, one-sheet, PPT slides. Do NOT use for: a single already-scoped piece of copy or a tone/voice check (use Backstage Writer instead); drafting the Campaign and Creative Brief document itself (use Brief Writer — B2C only; this skill assumes a brief/theme already exists); social performance benchmarking or peer comparison (use Social Pulse); any B2C asset request (not yet supported by this skill); recruitment or employer-brand content (out of scope).
