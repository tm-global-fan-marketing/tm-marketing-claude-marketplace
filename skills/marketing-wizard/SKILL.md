  Generator, which has no content yet, or a B2B creative brief, which no
  current skill covers). Do NOT use for: requests that already explicitly
  invoke a specific skill's own slash command (hand off directly instead);
  requests clearly outside the marketing plugin's domain (legal drafting, HR,
  unrelated engineering work); or as a re-classification trigger on every
  follow-up turn within an already-classified conversation — only re-route if
  the topic changes materially.
